Advanced / manual tools

① Pull a specific night (manual) — the Daily pull does this for you. To re-pull an arbitrary night by hand: set Night to pull (top of screen), tap FORCE_TRIM seek (rewinds the dump to that night), then Sync full history (drains it forward through the night). ⚠️ The drain frees the records — let the WHOOP app sync first.

② Flash data range — WHOOP states up to 14 days, but a real read showed this band holding ~44 days. Show oldest on flash reads the band's data-range marker (read-only — moves nothing) and reports the earliest date still on the band, so you know the oldest you can pick in the date box above.

Oldest on flash: — tap “Show oldest on flash”

③ Read-only checks (non-destructive) — neither acks or frees anything. Quick sync streams just the first window so you can see what's there. Check sync cursor reads the band's oldest-unsynced marker without changing it.

A/B the sync speed: tap both Speed tests (read-only, nothing freed) and compare the rec/s in the log. The toggle controls whether the real “Sync full history” uses high-freq.

Phase 2 · IMU / actigraphy — capture the raw int16 6-axis accel+gyro (WHOOP's “R21”) to unlock true sleep movement, steps & VO₂. Both are non-destructive. Realtime IMU: connect → tap on → hold flat & still 5s, tilt onto each edge/end/face 3s, then shake → tap off → Send to laptop. Historical probe: read-only, streams the first window looking for R21.

Settled: skin-temp & SpO₂ are decoded straight from the history pull (they show on Health Monitor). The remaining hunt is raw high-rate accel for Steps — the High-freq sync probe (cmd 96) is the best shot; tap it, then Send the capture. (GATT/IMU/Raw buttons are diagnostics.)
