How to pull the data — step by step
1
Quit the WHOOP app on your phone first. A band only talks to one device at a time, so if the official app is connected, we can't be.
2
Tap Connect WHOOP above and pick your band. Live heart rate should start showing on the other tabs straight away.
3
Tap Start capture (it turns green). This records every raw message the band sends, so nothing is lost.
4
Tap get_hello, then data_range, then Realtime HR (it turns on) — that asks the band to stream its live sensor data.
5
Watch fd4b frames count up for about 20 seconds. The more it climbs, the more data we get.
6
Tap Dump & copy. The captured data is copied to your clipboard (and shown in the box). Paste it to Claude — that's the bit we decode to teach the app real recovery / strain / sleep.
7
When done, send command 3 data 00 to stop the stream. Repeat over a few days (including after sleep) so we capture a full picture.
Collecting & comparing — recommendations

• Always wear the band while capturing — off the wrist there's no pulse, so the data is just noise.

• This app and the official WHOOP app take turns on Bluetooth — a band talks to one device at a time. Quit the WHOOP app before connecting here, and reopen it after.

• Keep using the WHOOP app normally otherwise. It keeps the band recording everything (including sleep) and its numbers are the answer key we calibrate against.

A
Live capture — wear it, quit the WHOOP app, connect, turn Realtime HR on, capture ~20 s, then Dump & copy.
B
Full day / sleep — live normally with the WHOOP app (wear it overnight). Later, quit the app, connect here, tap data_range, capture, then Dump & copy.
C
With every dump, note the WHOOP app's numbers for that same day — Recovery %, Day Strain, Sleep %, HRV (ms), Resting HR — and send them alongside the dump. That pairing is what calibrates the screens above to match WHOOP.
